Liquid Clock ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. 5. Children's Privacy

Our App does not address anyone under the age of 4. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and you are aware that your child has provided us with Personal Data, please contact us.
